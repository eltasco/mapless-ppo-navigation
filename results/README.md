# Recorded simulation results

- [`paired_benchmark_summary.csv`](paired_benchmark_summary.csv) and [`paired_benchmark_summary.json`](paired_benchmark_summary.json): **historical aggregates** for PPO versus the **untuned forward-only** field (not a calibrated classical baseline).
- [`reported_benchmark_output.txt`](reported_benchmark_output.txt): original console excerpt.
- [`recorded_benchmarks.csv`](recorded_benchmarks.csv): historical PPO-only results.

**The historical raw episode-level benchmark CSV/JSON were not included in the materials for this release.** They are not reconstructed from aggregate percentages. For the scope, uncertainty and instructions to generate and validate **new** per-episode evidence, consult [data provenance](../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md).
